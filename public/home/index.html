Why I built it

I ran a football academy for eight years. At its biggest it had 350 players a week across six venues.

Every month it was the same: a spreadsheet, a pile of bank transfers, and me messaging parents late at night to ask if they'd paid.

So I built the thing I wished I'd had. Parents book and pay themselves, memberships go out on the 1st, and I know who's paid without asking anyone.

I've sold my academy since. Player Portal is what I took from it.

What it does

Everything between "I'm interested" and "paid".

Built around how an academy actually runs: groups by age, terms, trials, camps and the 1st of the month.

  • Your booking pageClasses, ages, spaces left and prices. Parents book and pay in a couple of minutes, day or night.
  • Paid on the 1stMemberships collected automatically. Failed cards are retried and you can see who's behind.
  • Free trialsFamilies book a trial online, get a reminder, and get a one-tap link to join afterwards.
  • Holiday campsWhole weeks or single days, with places counted so you never overbook.
  • WaitlistsWhen a group's full, the next family is offered the place when it opens.
  • 1-2-1 sessionsCoach timetables, booking and payment, alongside your groups.
  • RegistersEvery class is its own register. Tick who came and see who's new or on a trial.
  • Welcome packsA PDF per class that new families get straight away. You see who opened it.
  • Your dashboardMonthly income, who's paying, who's at risk and today's sessions. You're told about every booking and payment.

Questions

Questions academies ask me.

Something else? Message me on Instagram and I'll answer it myself.

I use ClassForKids or Gymcatch. How hard is it to move?

Your families and memberships can be moved across for you. Members who've already paid for this month come across with their first charge on the next billing date, so nobody pays twice.

Will my parents be charged twice?

No. Anyone who's paid you for the month starts on the next billing date, and anyone holding prepaid credit keeps it.

How much of my time does it take?

About 15 minutes to connect payments and an hour to add your classes. Or have it done for you on the Ecosystem.

When do I get paid?

Payments go to your own Stripe account and on to your bank automatically. You can see every payment in your dashboard.

What if a parent's card fails?

It's retried automatically, the parent gets a link to pay, and you can see who's behind at a glance, so nobody slips through.

Is there a contract?

No. It's monthly, and you can cancel any time.
