Every week · the spine of the membership

The Monday call.

Forty-five minutes, live, the whole room. You bring your numbers. You leave with one thing to do before next Monday. It's the same link every week and there's nothing to book.

When and where

Mondays are the one day most academy owners aren't on the grass, and 12:30 leaves the morning for admin and the afternoon for school pick-ups. Put it in your diary as a standing meeting. If the time genuinely doesn't work for most of the room, say so on the call and we'll move it as a group.

01

How the 45 minutes run

Same shape every week, so you always know what's coming and what to bring.

0 to 10
Numbers round

Everyone, one line each: players this week, the number you said you'd move last Monday, did it move. No stories, no excuses. Thirty seconds a person. If you didn't do the thing, say "didn't do it" and we move on. Nobody's judging; the round is so the room can see who's stuck.

10 to 30
The lesson, or the hot seats

Odd weeks I teach one thing from the stage we're on. Even weeks it's hot seats: two or three of you bring a live problem and we work it in front of everyone. Both get recorded and filed in the library the same day.

30 to 40
Your one thing

Everyone names the single thing they'll do before next Monday. One, not five. I'll write them down and read them back at the top of next week's numbers round.

40 to 45
Close

What's landing in the library this week, who's doing a hot seat next week, and anything the room needs from each other. Then off.

03

What I ask of you

Bring numbers, not intentions.

Players this week, revenue this month, and the one number you said you'd move. Written down before the call, not worked out on it. The members who move fastest are the ones who bring evidence.

Camera on, if you can.

It's a room, not a webinar. It changes how honest people are, including you.

Take a hot seat within your first month.

Put your hand up in the WhatsApp group the week before. Nobody's problem is too small and nobody's is embarrassing. Every academy in the room has had it.

Do the one thing.

If you do nothing else between Mondays, do that. Twelve one-things in a row is how an academy changes.

04

Straight answers

What if I can't make a Monday?

The recording is in the library within a day, filed under the stage we covered. Post your numbers and your one thing in the WhatsApp group instead so you still get read back next week.

I'm on the grass at 12:30.

Say so in the group. If it's more than a couple of you we'll move the time. If it's just you, catch the recording and use the group for your one thing.

Can I bring my coach or business partner?

Yes, always. Hearing it from the room lands better than hearing it from you.

Is this the same as a one-to-one?

No. Mondays are the room. If you want me on your academy specifically, that's the Mastermind: a planning call and written plan, a one-to-one every month, and unstuck calls between them. Founding members keep their monthly one-to-one as before. Details on the Work with me page.
