# ASCEND — Member Delivery SOP

Your own checklist. The point of this document is that member #12 gets the same
standard as member #1, on a week when you're busy and can't rely on remembering.

Nothing here takes long. The whole first-hour block is about 25 minutes of real work.

---

## Trigger: someone pays

### Within the first hour

1. **Send the welcome email** — `WELCOME-EMAILS.md`, email 0. Personalise the first
   line with something specific from the sales call. It takes 90 seconds and it is
   the difference between "I've joined a thing" and "I've joined *John's* thing".
2. **Send the hub link and their access code** — same email.
3. **Add them to the member list** (Resend audience / your CRM), tagged `ascend-member`
   with their join date.
4. **Create their Drive folder** — `Members/[Academy Name]/` from the template below.
5. **Put a 30-day reminder in your calendar**, titled with their name. This is the one
   people skip, and it's the one that catches a member going quiet.

### Within 48 hours, if they haven't booked the kickoff

Send email 1 (the nudge). Don't wait a week. The single strongest predictor of
someone getting value is whether they book the kickoff in the first few days.

### The night before the kickoff call

- Read their intake form properly. Not skim — properly.
- Run their numbers through the pricing calculator yourself, before the call.
  Walking in already knowing their real hourly rate is the moment they realise
  this is different from every other coaching thing they've bought.
- Write down the one thing you think their answer is. You may be wrong; you
  usually won't be. Hold it back until section 4 of the call.

## The kickoff call — 45 minutes

Different call from the discovery/sales call. They've already bought. Do not sell.
The job here is to earn the £50 in the first 45 minutes and set one clear target.

**0–3 min · Frame it.**
"Forty-five minutes. I've read your intake, so we're not starting from scratch.
By the end we'll have agreed one thing you're fixing in the next month, and how
we'll know it worked. Sound alright?"

**3–12 min · Reflect their intake back.**
Say what you saw. Read their own numbers back at them. This proves you did the work
and it surfaces anything they got wrong or downplayed. Ask: "Anything in there that's
changed since you filled it in, or that you were being generous about?"

**12–22 min · The real hourly rate.**
Show them what you calculated. Let it land — don't rush to reassure them. Then:
"That's not a criticism, it's just the number. Nearly everyone's first one looks
like that. Mine did."

**22–32 min · Find the constraint.**
Their diagnostic gives you a weakest stage. Test whether you believe it. The question
that usually cracks it: *"If I could fix exactly one thing in your academy overnight,
and only one, which would make the others easier?"*

**32–40 min · Agree the one thing.**
One fix. Written down. With a number attached and a date. Say it out loud and make
them say it back — if they can't repeat it, it isn't clear enough yet.
Bad: "work on marketing." Good: "By 30 September, every new enquiry gets the same
three-message follow-up within 24 hours, and I'll know how many converted."

**40–45 min · Point them at their stage folder, book the next call.**
Book the monthly call *while you're still on this call*. Never leave it to email.

### Within two hours of the call

Send email 2 (the same-day one action). Two hours, while it's still live in their
head. This is in `ascend-mentee-email-sequence.md` and it works — don't improvise it.

## The monthly rhythm

**Day 30 · First monthly call.** Ask for the three things in order: what you did,
what the number did, what got in the way. If the answer to all three is vague, that
is the actual problem and it's worth spending the whole call on.

**Every month after.** Same 30 minutes. Same three questions. The consistency is the
product — most owners have never had anyone ask them the same question twice.

**Quarterly.** Get them to retake the diagnostic and compare it to their week-one
score. Movement in a stage score is the most motivating evidence you can show
someone, and it costs you nothing to produce.

---

## The warning signs

Act on these rather than hoping. All three mean the same thing: reach out personally,
by voice or WhatsApp, not by email.

- **No kickoff booked after 7 days.** They've bought and stalled. Very recoverable now,
  much less so at week four.
- **A monthly call missed and not rebooked.** The month-four flat spot is real and it
  is where people quietly drift. Email 3 in the sequence exists for exactly this.
- **Nothing done between two consecutive calls.** Not a motivation problem — usually
  the agreed action was too big. Cut it in half and re-agree it.

## Member Drive folder template

Each member gets `Members/[Academy Name]/` containing:

- `Intake — [Academy].pdf` — their submitted intake
- `Call notes.doc` — one running document, newest at the top, dated
- `Their numbers.sheet` — baseline at join, updated each quarter
- `Diagnostic scores.sheet` — week 1, then each quarter
- `Their work/` — whatever they send you to look at

The running call-notes doc matters more than it looks. Being able to open a call with
"last time you said the Thursday session was the one bleeding players" is the whole game.

---

## What this SOP is protecting against

At four members you can hold all of this in your head. At fifteen you cannot, and the
failure mode isn't dramatic — nobody complains. They just quietly get a worse version
of the thing, stop booking calls, and cancel in month five while telling you it's fine.
