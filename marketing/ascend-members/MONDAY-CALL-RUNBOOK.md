# The Monday Call — John's runbook

The weekly live call is the spine of the £100 ASCEND Academy. It is the thing that
stops the membership being a Drive folder, and it is the cheapest retention move on
Louise Brough's list. This is how to set it up once and run it every week without it
eating the day.

Member-facing version: `monday-call.html` (agenda, 12-week plan, rules).

## Set-up (once, about 30 minutes)

1. **Zoom recurring meeting.** Zoom → Schedule → "ASCEND Monday Call", Recurring,
   Weekly, Monday 12:30, 45 min, no end date. Turn ON: waiting room off, mute on
   entry, **record to cloud automatically**, allow participants to unmute. Copy the
   join link.
2. **Paste the link** into `MONDAY_CALL_LINK` in `monday-call.html` (×1) and
   `work-with-me.html` (×1). Optional: export the Zoom .ics and put its URL in
   `MONDAY_CALL_ICS` in `monday-call.html`, or delete that "Add to calendar" button.
3. **Drive folder.** In the Academy Owners Library create `07 MONDAY CALLS`. Inside it,
   one folder per quarter (`2026 Q4`). Recordings go there, named
   `YYYY-MM-DD · Wk N · Stage · Title.mp4`. Add a row in `library.html` when the first
   one lands.
4. **WhatsApp group.** Create "ASCEND Members" (group, not community), you as admin,
   add the founders. Pin one message: the Zoom link, the time, and "post your numbers +
   one thing here if you miss a Monday".
5. **Calendar.** Block Monday 12:00–13:30 in your own calendar forever. 12:00–12:30 is
   prep, 13:15–13:30 is the post-call.

## Before the call (Monday 12:00, 15 minutes)

- Open last week's **one-things list** (a running note, one line per member per week).
- Check the group for anyone who posted numbers because they can't make it.
- Have the week's material open: the calculator, the Offers Bank PDF, a member's
  numbers for a hot seat. See the 12-week plan below.
- Hot-seat weeks: confirm the two or three names in the group by Sunday evening.

## Running it (45 minutes, same every week)

| Time | Block | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Numbers round | Read back last week's one-things. Each member, 30 seconds: players this week, the number, did it move. Keep it moving: "Next." Write the answers on the list. |
| 10–30 | Lesson (odd weeks) or hot seats (even weeks) | Lesson: one idea, one member's real numbers on screen, 15 minutes max, 5 for questions. Hot seat: 7 minutes each — 2 min the member states the problem with numbers, 3 min the room asks questions, 2 min you give the one move. |
| 30–40 | One thing | Go round. One thing each, specific enough to check next week ("email the 14 lapsed parents the £5 trial offer", not "work on marketing"). Write it down. |
| 40–45 | Close | What lands in the library this week. Who's in the hot seat next Monday. Stop on time. Stopping on time is the habit. |

Rules you hold, every week: start at 12:30 whether five are there or fifty; no
stories in the numbers round; one thing means one; stop at 13:15.

## After the call (13:15, 15 minutes)

1. Zoom cloud recording → download → Drive `07 MONDAY CALLS / quarter`. Name it.
2. Post in the WhatsApp group: three lines. What we covered, the link to the recording,
   next week's hot seats. Same format every time.
3. Add the week's one-things to the running note. This note is also your best
   case-study source: after 12 weeks you have every member's numbers, weekly.
4. Last Monday of the month: copy the month's numbers per member into a single sheet
   (players, revenue). That sheet is where "before and after" testimonials come from.

## The 12-week rotation

Two weeks per ASCEND stage: lesson, then hot seats. Every fourth week the numbers round
extends into the month check-in (10 minutes instead of 5, everyone gives players +
revenue + the monthly number). Week 12 is the quarter review, and the room votes on
where the next 12 weeks go deepest.

| Wk | Stage | Type | Topic | Material to have open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Audit | Lesson | Your real hourly rate | Pricing calculator, one volunteer's costs |
| 2 | A Audit | Hot seats | The numbers nobody says out loud | Three members' hourly rates |
| 3 | S Sell | Lesson | Pricing and the offer | Offers Bank PDF, memberships vs PAYG |
| 4 | — | **Check-in** + hot seats | Numbers month; a price rise or offer that isn't landing | Month sheet |
| 5 | C Capacity | Lesson | Filling sessions | 15-slot content plan, trial funnel, parent scripts |
| 6 | C Capacity | Hot seats | A session that won't fill | Class, time, price, 4 weeks of numbers |
| 7 | E Expand | Lesson | More money from the players you have | Offers Bank upsell map (camps, 1-2-1s, siblings) |
| 8 | — | **Check-in** + hot seats | Numbers month; a camp or second venue decision | Month sheet |
| 9 | N Normalise | Lesson | Getting it out of your head | Academy Bible, Academy on a Page |
| 10 | N Normalise | Hot seats | The thing only you can do | Members' time audits |
| 11 | D Delegate | Lesson | Hiring your first coach properly | Role profiles, 90-day plan, your own story |
| 12 | — | **Quarter review** | 12 weeks of numbers side by side; vote on next quarter | Month sheet ×3 |

The "two trainings a month" promised on the sales page are the two lesson weeks in
each four-week block. The recorded hot seats count as bonus material, not the two.

## When it wobbles

- **Three people turn up.** Run it exactly the same. The recording is the product for
  everyone else, and a small room gives better hot seats. Do not cancel; cancelling
  once teaches the room that Mondays are optional.
- **You can't make a Monday.** Post the lesson as a 10-minute Loom into the group by
  12:30 and ask for numbers + one thing in replies. Never silently skip.
- **Nobody volunteers for hot seats.** Pick two from the numbers round: whoever's
  number didn't move for two weeks running. Say it kindly and do it anyway.
- **A member dominates.** "Good, hold that for your hot seat next week. Next."
