How it works Read each statement and score yourself 1–5 (1 = strongly disagree / not happening at all · 5 = strongly agree / consistent and dialled in). Be brutally honest — if you've "kind of" got something but it's inconsistent, that's a 2 or 3, not a 4. Score every statement, even for stages that feel like they're not relevant yet. Takes about 10 minutes.

2. Focus on your lowest-scoring stage, not your overall stage.

Your stage totals are the fastest way for us to agree what to work on. If you have been stuck on the same stage for six months or more, say so — that is a different conversation. Book a call.
