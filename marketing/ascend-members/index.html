02

The six stages

Every academy problem sits in one of these. The diagnostic tells you which one is holding you back — work that one, not the one that feels most urgent.

A
Audit
Do you actually know your numbers?
S
Sell
Are you filling sessions consistently?
C
Capacity
Can your operation handle growth?
E
Expand
Are you growing the business, or just the workload?
N
Normalise
Does your business run the same way every time?
D
Delegate
Are you the owner, or still the operator?
Don't guess

The diagnostic scores you out of 25 on each stage and tells you your weakest one. It takes about 10 minutes and it is the single most useful thing you can do in week one.
