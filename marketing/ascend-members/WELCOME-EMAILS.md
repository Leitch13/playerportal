# ASCEND — Onboarding Emails (the first 30 days)

These are the emails that didn't exist yet: the ones between "they paid" and
"they're in the rhythm". They stop the silence that kills a new membership.

**This supplements your existing `ascend-mentee-email-sequence.md`** (in Downloads) —
it does not replace it. That file has the session-based emails and they're good.
The hand-off point is marked below.

Same principle as the existing sequence: **one action per email.** After a good sales
call the urge is to send everything at once. Everything at once is nothing at once.

Gaps to fill: `[Name]`, `[Academy]`, `[hub link]`, `[booking link]`, `[access code]`.

---

## 0 · Day 0 — the welcome

*Send within an hour of payment. The single most important email in the sequence:
it's the one that decides whether they feel like they joined a thing or joined you.
Personalise the opening line with something specific they said on the call — it takes
90 seconds and it changes the whole tone.*

**Subject:** You're in — here's where everything lives

Hi [Name],

Welcome to ASCEND. Good to have [Academy] in.

[One specific line from your call — "Still thinking about what you said about the
Thursday session" / "Right, let's get that pricing sorted."]

**Everything lives here:** [hub link]
Your access code is **[access code]**. Bookmark it — it's the only link you need.

When you open it you'll find a 30-day path with nine steps on it. Work through it in
order. It front-loads the boring part — getting honest about your numbers — because
every good decision after that depends on knowing where you actually are.

**Two things to do this week, in this order:**

1. **Book your kickoff call** — [booking link]. Forty-five minutes, just us. Book it
   now rather than when you've "had a look round". Slots go a week or two out, and
   having the date in the diary is what makes the rest of the week happen.
2. **Fill in the intake form** — it's step 2 on the path. Ten minutes. It means I turn
   up already knowing your academy and we spend the call on what to do, rather than on
   you explaining the setup.

Estimates are fine on the intake. If you knew all those numbers exactly you probably
wouldn't need me.

One thing worth saying now: the members who get the most out of this are the ones who
message me when they're stuck rather than waiting for the next call. That's what the
WhatsApp number is for. Nothing is too small.

Talk soon,
John

## 1 · Day 2–3 — the nudge, only if no kickoff booked

*Do not skip this and do not wait a week. Whether someone books the kickoff in the
first few days is the strongest signal you'll get about how the whole thing goes.*

**Subject:** grab a slot before the week runs away

Hi [Name],

Noticed the kickoff call isn't in the diary yet. No problem — but grab a slot now
rather than later, because "when things calm down" is a week that never turns up in
this business. I've been waiting for it since about 2014.

[booking link]

Forty-five minutes. Bring nothing. If you've filled the intake in beforehand we'll get
much further, but if you haven't, come anyway and we'll do it live.

John

---

## 2 · Day 7 — the first-week check

*Only if they've booked but gone quiet on the path. Skip it if they're moving.*

**Subject:** how's the first week been?

Hi [Name],

A week in. No pressure attached to this — just checking the path made sense and you
didn't hit anything confusing.

If you've done the diagnostic, one thing worth knowing: **work your lowest-scoring
stage, not your overall level.** Nearly everyone's instinct is to go at the stage that
feels most urgent, which is usually Sell. If your Audit score is the low one, more
marketing will just get you more of a thing that isn't paying you properly.

If you haven't started yet, that's fine too. Say so and we'll start on the call.

John

## 3 · Day 14 — the halfway nudge

*Between the kickoff and the first monthly call. Short. Purely to keep the agreed
action alive while there's still time to do something about it.*

**Subject:** halfway to the call

Hi [Name],

Two weeks since we spoke, two until the next one. The thing we agreed was:

**[the one thing, in their own words]**

If it's done — good, tell me and I'll stop nagging. If it's half done, that's normal.
If it hasn't started, that's worth ten minutes of thought, because it usually means
one of two things: either it was too big, or it wasn't actually the right thing.

Both are fixable and both are much easier to fix now than on the call. Message me.

John

---

## 4 · Day 28 — bring three things

*Two days before the first monthly call. Sets the standard for every call after it.*

**Subject:** three things for Thursday

Hi [Name],

Our call is [day, time]. Bring three things:

1. **What you did.**
2. **What the number did.**
3. **What got in the way.**

That's the format every month, so it's worth getting used to it now. "It didn't work"
is a completely fine answer to number two as long as you know why — that's information
and we can use it. The only version that wastes the half hour is turning up with
nothing and reconstructing the month from memory while I wait.

Two minutes of prep. See you [day].

John

---

## 5 · Day 90 — retake the diagnostic

*The retention email. Nothing sells the value of staying like seeing your own numbers
move — and this costs you nothing to send.*

**Subject:** do the diagnostic again

Hi [Name],

Three months in. Worth retaking the diagnostic: [hub link]

Ten minutes, and this time you've got a score to compare it to. Dig out your week-one
total before you start — the comparison is the point, and it's the clearest evidence
you'll get that the dull months were doing something.

Fair warning: some stages go *down* on a retake. That's usually not a decline, it's
you having learned enough to answer honestly. It happened to me.

Bring both scores to the next call.

John

## Cadence summary

| When | Email | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Welcome + hub access | Always |
| Day 2–3 | Booking nudge | Only if no kickoff booked |
| Day 7 | First-week check | Only if gone quiet |
| Kickoff + 2h | "The one thing" | Always — *existing sequence, email 1* |
| Day 14 | Halfway nudge | Always |
| Day 28 | Bring three things | Always |
| ~Week 4 | "Nothing's happening" | If flat — *existing sequence, email 2* |
| Day 90 | Retake the diagnostic | Always |

After day 90 the existing sequence takes over: pillar transitions and the
no-agenda check-in, sent as the relationship warrants rather than on a timer.
