04

Straight answers

How long before I see anything change?

Pricing changes can show up in a month. Marketing takes two to three before the numbers are trustworthy. Delegation and systems take six, and they're the ones that actually change your life. Anyone promising faster than that is selling you something.

What if I fall behind?

There's nothing to fall behind. There's no cohort and no timetable — the 30-day path is a suggested order, not a deadline. Pick it up when your season allows.

Can I cancel?

Any time, no notice period and no exit conversation you have to sit through. Tell me and it stops at the end of that month.

My academy is tiny / not launched yet. Am I wasting your time?

No, and it's a better position than it feels like — you get to build it properly instead of unpicking years of decisions made in a hurry. We just start at the plan rather than the business.

Do I have to use Player Portal?

No. It's my software and it's what I'd use, but none of the coaching depends on it. Plenty of members run on something else.
