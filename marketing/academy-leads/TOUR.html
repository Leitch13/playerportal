4

The coach is trained — and kept

Hiring is act 3. Most academies stop there. The OS keeps going: training lives in one link, compliance renews itself, and good coaches get asked how they're doing before they leave.

Day oneand forever

The staff hub — one read-only link Ryan bookmarks on his phone. No logins, no per-seat cost, never stale:

Resources · Coaching: session plans (Canva links — always latest version) · Admin: handbook PDF, "your first session here" video · Playbooks: the 5 cards · Safeguarding: policy PDFs

Canva view links mean you edit the design once and every coach sees the update. Videos are Loom / unlisted YouTube. PDFs attach directly.

30 days beforeany cert expires

Renewal warning — a formula watches Ryan's PVG, first-aid and safeguarding dates; both he and the owner get told. Automation 13

To: owner@academy.comSubject: Cert expiring in 30 days: Ryan Ferguson

One of Ryan's certificates (PVG / first aid / safeguarding) expires in 30 days — check the Cert watch view and get the renewal booked.

7 days before

The red flag. Automation 14

3 / 6 / 12 monthsfrom start date

The bad day

Wednesday session. Max goes down awkwardly — or worse, a child tells a coach something that can't wait. Good events get automations; bad events get a rehearsed reaction. The coach never has to improvise.

At the pitchthe human bit comes FIRST here

The coach follows the laminated playbook card from the kit bag — injury: stop, assess, treat, tell the parent. The reaction was decided on a calm Tuesday, printed, and rehearsed at a staff meeting. Nobody improvises on the worst day.

Next day, 9am

Under the hood

Everything above runs on one Airtable base, four one-file pages, and ~24 pre-written automations. Free tier. The client duplicates their own copy and owns it forever. Built once from three build docs; each client sets it up in an afternoon from the setup guide.

The client setup guide →  The sales sheet →
TableWhat it holdsWhich acts use it
LeadsEvery enquiry from every front door; the pipeline board. The original Lead Machine table, unchanged.Acts 1–2
MembersEnrolled players: joined date, fee, DOB, status, cancel reason. Created automatically from won leads.Acts 2, 6, 7
StaffCoaches from Applied to Active: quals, availability, three cert expiry dates + a days-to-expiry formula, start date.Acts 3–4
Onboarding tasksChecklist rows auto-created per hire, linked to the coach, with due dates.Acts 3, 7
ResourcesThe staff hub: Canva links, PDFs, videos, playbook cards. Shared to staff as one read-only view link.Act 4
IncidentsThe accident & incident log, fed by the phone form; safeguarding checklist lives on the record.Acts 5, 7
SessionsThe weekly timetable: venue, day, age group, lead coach. Reference data the emails pull from.Supporting
ScoreboardOne auto-filled row per month: leads, joins, cancels, actives, est. revenue.Act 7

Deliberately not in the base: payments and bookings. Whatever the academy uses for money (Player Portal, standing orders, anything) stays where it is — the OS records the fee number for the scoreboard and never touches a card. That keeps the template universal.
