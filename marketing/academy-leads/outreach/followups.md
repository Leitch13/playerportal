# Cold-email follow-ups — templates

Voice: John's. Short, plain, no "just checking in". Each one is a REPLY in the
original thread (so the first email sits underneath), signed with the phone.

## Day 4 — "one line"
Subject: (reply in thread)

Hi {first_name_or_there},

One line on the email below: I meant the offer. If you send me your
current schedule I'll put {academy_short} into Player Portal, with your
classes, prices and branding, so you can see it with your own stuff in
rather than a demo. Takes me a day, costs you nothing.

If it's not for you, a "no thanks" saves us both a chase.

John

## Day 10 — "last one"
Subject: (reply in thread)

Hi {first_name_or_there},

Last one from me, then I'll leave you alone.

{hook_reminder}

If the timing's wrong, tell me when's better and I'll come back then.
If it's never, no hard feelings.

John
07595426746

Notes for whoever runs this:
- {hook_reminder} = ONE sentence restating the specific thing noticed in the
  first email (e.g. "You're selling monthly packages through a tool built for
  pay-as-you-go, and that's the bit I can fix in a day."). Pull it from the
  first email in the thread; never generic.
- Never follow up a thread that has ANY reply from the academy, a bounce, or
  a "wrong email" apology (Cammy, no.1gkacademy).
- Never follow up existing customers or demo/test addresses.
