The bill of materials

What it takes to build

No servers, no code beyond the branded pages, no monthly stack cost. Four HTML pages, one Airtable base, about two dozen automations — then it's a template any academy duplicates in half an hour.

4

Branded front-door pages

Trial funnel (already built), coach application, incident report, general enquiry. Each is one HTML file with a single CONFIG block — name, colour, logo, offer.

1

Airtable base, 8 tables

Leads · Members · Staff · Onboarding tasks · Sessions · Resources · Incidents · Scoreboard. All linked, all on the free tier.

~24

Automations

The invisible staff: alerts, auto-replies, chase sequences, expiry reminders, the three briefings. All native Airtable — no Zapier, no Make.

1

Playbook card pack

One-page PDFs per scenario — injury, safeguarding, missing child, angry parent, coach no-show — drawn from the Academy Bible, loaded into the staff hub.

£0/mo

Running cost

Airtable free tier + free video hosting (Loom / unlisted YouTube) + Canva links. SMS stays a paid bolt-on for later.

30 min

Client setup

Duplicate the template base, fill four CONFIG blocks, paste two webhook URLs, send one test lead. Same handoff pattern as the Lead Machine.
