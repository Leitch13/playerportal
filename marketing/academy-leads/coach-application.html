Before you apply

Quick questions

Do I need coaching badges?
No. Attitude and reliability come first — plenty of our best coaches started with none. If you’re the right fit, we’ll help you get qualified.
Do I need a background check (PVG / DBS)?
Yes — everyone who coaches children does. Don’t worry about arranging it: it’s part of our onboarding and we walk you through it. You can’t take a group alone until it clears.
Is it paid?
Yes — per session, at a rate we’ll agree before you start, paid on time. This is a proper job, not “volunteering with expenses.”
How many hours a week?
As flexible as your availability. Some coaches do one evening a week, some build a near full-time diary across sessions, schools and holiday camps. Tell us what works in the form.
