1. Your funnel page

A branded page where parents claim a free trial. Your name, colours, offer.

📊

Every enquiry lands on a board you move from New → Booked → Enrolled — plus a Members table that fills itself when you win.

⚡

3. Nine automations

Instant alerts, instant replies, follow-up chasing — and trial confirmations, day-before reminders, no-show rescues and the welcome pack, all hands-off.

Important

It works with your booking system

This doesn't replace how you take bookings or payments (Player Portal, Spond, Coacha, Eventbrite, your own site — whatever you use). Your booking system captures the parents who finish booking. This captures everyone who was interested — and chases the ones who didn't book yet. Pick how it hands parents over:

Recommended

Mode A — Capture first, then book

Parent → your funnel → saved as a lead → "Book your time" → your booking system

You get the lead before they book — so even if they don't finish, you have their details and the system chases them. This is what makes it a leads system, not just a link.

Simpler

Mode B — Straight to booking

Parent → your funnel → button goes straight to your booking system

The page is just a prettier front door. Easier, but you only "have" the parents your booking system captures — you lose the ones who drop off.

No booking system yet? Leave the booking link blank. Parents get a "we'll be in touch" message, you get an instant alert, and you book them in yourself. The funnel + tracker is your system.

Do this once

The setup — 6 steps

1

Copy your leads tracker

Open the tracker link we sent you and click "Copy base". You now have your own private board — nobody else can see your leads. It comes with the Pipeline board, a Members table, and all nine automations pre-written.

  • Tracker link: open your leads tracker →
2

Make the funnel page yours

Open your funnel file and fill in the short CONFIG block at the bottom — it's all plain English:

  • Academy name, town, brand colour, logo
  • Your offer (free trial is the default) and the age range you accept
  • Your email — where alerts go
  • Your booking link — paste your booking system's URL here (Mode A). Leave blank for Mode B / no system.
⚠ Don't paste any passwords or keys into this page — it's public. Only the details above.
3

Connect the page to your tracker

In your tracker, grab the connection link (we've pre-built it — open Automations → the first one shows the link). Paste it into the funnel's formEndpoint field. That's the pipe that carries each new lead into your board.

4

Set your email on the alerts

In the tracker's Automations, two of them email you (new-lead alert + daily follow-up list). Pop your own email address into those, and switch all the automations On. They're already written — you can tweak the wording if you like.

5

Personalise the trial-to-member emails

First, pick your voice. Open the Email Voice Pack — every parent email written three ways (local & friendly, professional, community club). Read version one of each out loud; the one that sounds like something you'd actually say is yours. Copy those six into the automations. Then fill the three blanks:

  • Your enrol / booking link — goes in the "How did they find it?" email (the one that lands the morning after a trial)
  • The welcome pack — four lines in the welcome email: kit list, term dates, how payment works, your parents' WhatsApp group link
  • Your standard monthly fee — the default on the Members table
💡 From now on your only job after a session is dragging cards to Trial attended or No-show — the emails handle themselves either way.
6

Test it, then go live

Fill in your own funnel form as if you were a parent, then walk your test lead down the whole board. Check five things:

  • A new card appears on your board
  • You get the "new lead" email; the test parent (you) gets the auto-reply
  • Drag to Trial booked (set a trial date) → confirmation email arrives
  • Drag to Trial attended → the "how did it go?" email arrives with your enrol link
  • Drag to Enrolled → a row appears in Members and the welcome pack sends

All five working? Delete the test lead, publish the funnel page (or paste it into your website) and share the link. You're live.

Once it's running

Your 2-minute daily routine

The system does the chasing. Your only job is to move cards and make the calls.

New lead comes in → you get an email in seconds. Call or message them while they're hot.

Open your Pipeline board each morning → drag each card along: New → Contacted → Trial booked → Trial attended (or No-show) → Enrolled.

The system handles the rest → trial confirmations, day-before reminders, morning-after follow-ups, no-show rescues and the welcome pack all send themselves.

Nothing goes cold → each morning you get a "chase these today" email of anyone waiting on you — including trial kids whose parents went quiet.
