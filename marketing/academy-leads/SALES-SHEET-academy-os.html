The fix

Every event gets a pre-decided reaction

That's the whole idea. Something happens in your academy — good or bad — and the system either handles it automatically or tells the right person exactly what to do.

When a lead comes in

It gets captured & chased

Instant alert to you, instant reply to the parent, follow-ups until they book or say no. (That's the Lead Machine — it's module one.)

When a trial happens

It gets converted

Confirmation, day-before reminder, next-morning "how was it?" with the enrol link, no-show rescue, automatic welcome pack.

When a coach applies

They get hired properly

Application page → pipeline → offer triggers the full onboarding checklist: contract, PVG, first aid, kit, handbook. Nobody coaches un-cleared.

When a coach starts

They get trained & kept

One staff-hub link: session plans, walkthrough videos, your handbook. Cert renewals chased automatically. Check-in prompts at 3, 6 and 12 months.

When something goes wrong

There's a playbook

Laminated cards for injury, safeguarding, missing child, angry parent. A 60-second phone report becomes your official incident log — and safeguarding steps can't be quietly dropped.

When a member leaves

They get won back

Reason logged, graceful exit email, automatic win-back offer 30 days later — and you finally see why people leave.
