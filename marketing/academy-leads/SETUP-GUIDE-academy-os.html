Do this once

The setup — 7 steps

1

Copy your HQ board

Open the link we sent and click "Copy base". You now own a private copy — your leads, members, staff and incidents are visible to nobody else.

  • HQ board link: open your Academy HQ →
2

Brand your trial funnel

Open the funnel file and fill the plain-English CONFIG block: academy name, colour, logo, offer, your email, your booking link. Full detail is in the Lead Machine guide — this is the same page.

⚠ Never paste passwords or keys into any of the four pages — they're public.
3

Brand the other two pages

Same trick, two more files, five minutes each:

  • Coach application — fill in the four "why coach with us" perks while you're in there. They're your hiring pitch.
  • Incident report — just name, colour, logo. The playbook wording is already written.
4

Connect the pipes

Each page has a formEndpoint line; your HQ board has a matching connection link for each (Automations → the webhook at the top of each one). Paste trial→trial, coach→coach, incident→incident. Three pastes, done.

5

Put your email on the alerts, switch everything On

The automations are pre-written. Anywhere an email goes to the owner, make it yours (new lead, new applicant, incident alert, the daily briefing, the Monday pulse, the monthly scoreboard). Then switch all automations On.

6

Load your staff hub & print the playbook cards

The Resources table is your staff hub — coaches see it through one read-only link they bookmark. Load it before your first hire:

  • Your coach handbook (PDF)
  • 2–3 session plans (PDF or a Canva view link — updates itself forever)
  • A short "your first session here" phone video
  • The five playbook cards (PDF) — then print and laminate a set for the kit bag, with the incident page's QR code and your welfare officer's number filled in
7

Run the test day, then go live

Pretend to be everyone for ten minutes: submit a fake lead, book and attend its trial, apply as a fake coach, make the offer, file a fake incident from your phone, cancel a fake member. Watch the board fill and the emails land. Delete the fakes, publish the pages, share the links. You're live.

Once it's running

Your 5-minute daily routine

The system runs the academy's reactions. You make decisions and phone calls.

8am: read one email. Who to chase, trials today, onboarding overdue, certs expiring, open incidents. If it says "everything's handled" — it is.

Drag cards after sessions. Trial attended or no-show; applicant to offer; tick onboarding tasks. Every drag fires the right emails for you.

Make the calls the system queues. The hot lead, the day-after-injury check-in, the coach's 3-month coffee. It remembers; you ring.

Monday & the 1st: your numbers arrive. Leads, trials, joins, cancellations — the scoreboard a mentor would ask for, built while you slept.
